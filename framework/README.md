## Single claim

The capacity-allocation bound `k_max = B / [(1−φ)c + φσ]`
generalizes to a structured framework: a three-mechanism taxonomy
distinguishing scaling regimes, a projection-class taxonomy
sub-dividing mechanism-3 by per-coordinatee state dimensionality,
a per-direction multi-channel generalization with a
φ-bimodality theorem, a cross-depth propagation form coupling
neighboring tower depths, an architectural lever raising φ to
replace c with σ, and a 19-row cross-mechanism table across
chemistry, biology, neural systems, software, distributed
systems, multi-agent AI, organizational management, macro policy,
and modern ML architectures.

## Relationship to the convergence paper

The convergence paper makes a narrow empirical claim
(substrate-invariant ceiling k̄ ∈ [4, 10] for open-interface
coordinators with rich-state coordinatees) supported by eight
substrates and an own-collected probing experiment. This paper
develops the structural framework that organizes the convergence:
the three-mechanism taxonomy, the projection-class extensions
(P_3-chunked at 10–30, P_3-recognition at 100–500), the
per-direction generalization, and the cross-depth propagation
form. The two papers are decoupled: the convergence paper stands
alone with the empirical core; the framework paper stands alone
with the structural extensions.
