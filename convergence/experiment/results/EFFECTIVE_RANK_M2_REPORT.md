# M2 — Effective Rank at Variance Thresholds (Cross-Architecture)

**STATUS: NON-PRE-REGISTERED EXPLORATORY ANALYSIS,
4/4 POSITIVE RESULT.**

Generated by `src/14_effective_rank_m2.py`. Operates on all
four probed models' concept-probe activations (4000-sample
subset per layer, deterministic seed 42).

This is the M2 candidate method from `OPTIMAL_K_REPORT.md` —
designed specifically for high-PR continuous-manifold regimes
where vanilla k-means + silhouette has no power.

## Method

For each model and each layer:

1. Mean-center the activations and compute the singular-value
   spectrum (raw geometry).
2. L2-normalize each sample's activation vector to unit length,
   then mean-center, then compute the singular-value spectrum
   (cosine geometry — magnitude information removed).
3. For variance thresholds p ∈ {0.50, 0.75, 0.90, 0.95, 0.99}:
   record the number of principal components needed to explain
   p of the total variance.

The participation ratio (Section 4.5 follow-up #2) is one
summary number from this spectrum. M2 reports the full curve
across thresholds, which catches structure PR can blur.

## Result — 4/4 inverted-U at every variance threshold

| Metric × Threshold | Inverted-U / 4 models | Mean middle/late ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Raw, n for 90% variance | **4 / 4** | 2.03 |
| Raw, n for 95% variance | **4 / 4** | 1.66 |
| Raw, n for 99% variance | **4 / 4** | 1.29 |
| Cosine, n for 90% variance | **4 / 4** | 2.02 |
| Cosine, n for 95% variance | **4 / 4** | 1.65 |
| Cosine, n for 99% variance | **4 / 4** | 1.29 |

Per-model peak vs late, raw geometry, n needed for 90% variance:

| Model | Peak layer (norm. depth) | Peak n@90% | Late n@90% | Peak / Late |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Llama-3.2-3B | layer 8 (0.29) | 884 | 156 | 5.7× |
| Mistral-7B-v0.3 | layer 12 (0.38) | 1133 | 129 | 8.8× |
| Qwen2.5-7B | layer 12 (0.43) | 843 | 13 | 65× |
| Llama-3.1-8B | layer 12 (0.38) | 1087 | 138 | 7.9× |

The middle of every model needs **5.7× to 65× more PCA
components** to reach 90% variance than the late layers do.
This is a sharper signal than the participation-ratio
inverted-U (3-4× middle/late ratio) because it counts
*significant* directions rather than averaging over the full
spectrum.

The signal weakens monotonically with threshold: at 99%
variance the middle/late ratio averages 1.29× (still
inverted-U in 4/4 models, but smaller). The middle's "extra"
structure lives in the *significant* variance directions, not
in the long tail of small principal components.

## Cosine geometry vs raw — magnitude does not do the work

Raw and cosine results are within ~5% of each other at every
threshold for every model. The dimensionality inversion is
preserved when magnitude information is removed by L2-row-
normalization. This means the inversion is fundamentally
about **direction count**, not magnitude variation across
the activation manifold — which is what the P_3-chunked
sub-class hypothesis predicts (more roughly-orthogonal
concept axes simultaneously held in the middle).

## What this establishes

This converts the within-transformer P_3-chunked hypothesis
from "unfalsified, awaiting appropriate methods" (the
OPTIMAL_K_REPORT framing) to **"4/4 cross-architecture
positive result under M2"**.

Together with the three measurements in
`MIDDLE_LAYER_GEOMETRY_REPORT.md`, `CLUSTER_PURITY_REPORT.md`,
and the pre-registered concept-probe peak (4.5.3 confirmed),
the within-transformer signature now has *four* orthogonal
geometric measurements all replicating 4/4 across architectures
— except M2 is more accurately described as a *refinement* of
the participation-ratio finding (same underlying claim, finer-
grained operationalization) rather than a new orthogonal
measurement. Listing them by what they capture:

1. **Linear separability** (probe accuracy): uniform across
   layers from layer 1.
2. **Cluster dominance** (NMI): inverted-U; perimeter-only.
3. **Effective dimensionality** (participation ratio): 3-4×
   middle/late.
4. **Effective rank at variance thresholds** (M2): refines (3),
   showing 5.7× to 65× middle/late at 90% variance, with the
   gap narrowing as threshold approaches 100%.

## What this analysis does NOT establish

- The four operationalizations are mutually consistent, not
  independent. They measure related geometric properties of
  the same activation manifold.
- The within-transformer P_3-chunked claim is stronger than
  before, but still a *substrate-internal* finding. The
  paper's substrate-invariant claim (Section 2.6) is supported
  *across* substrates by Dunbar's nested rings, not by this
  single-substrate within-LLM measurement.
- M2 was selected because the cluster-k* test ruled out
  k-means + silhouette (OPTIMAL_K_REPORT). Selection bias on
  method choice is real and acknowledged: M2 is the natural
  next method to try given what failed; we are not
  pre-registering its use.
